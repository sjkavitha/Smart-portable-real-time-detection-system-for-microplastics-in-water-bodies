# 🌊 Low-Cost Portable Microplastic Detection System

 A low-cost and portable microplastic detection system using Optical Sensing, Embedded Systems, and Image Processing for real-time water quality monitoring.

---

## 📖 Overview

Microplastic pollution has become one of the most serious environmental concerns due to its adverse effects on aquatic ecosystems and human health. Conventional laboratory techniques such as FTIR and Raman Spectroscopy provide accurate results but require expensive equipment, skilled personnel, and laboratory infrastructure.

This project presents a Low-Cost Portable Microplastic Detection System that estimates microplastic particles in water using optical sensing, embedded processing, and sensor fusion. The developed prototype provides a portable, affordable, and efficient solution suitable for environmental monitoring and academic research.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Detect microplastic particles in water samples.
- Develop a low-cost portable prototype.
- Perform real-time particle analysis.
- Estimate particle count, particle size, and concentration.
- Provide a simple and user-friendly monitoring system.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

1. Water sample is introduced into the transparent flow chamber.
2. White LED illuminates the water sample.
3. LDR, turbidity, and ultrasonic sensors acquire measurement data.
4. ESP32 collects and processes all sensor values.
5. The system estimates particle count, particle size, concentration, and scattering level.
6. The analyzed results are displayed on the LCD screen.
7. The monitoring process repeats automatically at regular intervals.
